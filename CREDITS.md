## Instrument artwork (assets/*.png) – all 16 instruments
All 16 cartoon instrument drawings were **supplied by the teacher (Charles)**. All rights remain with him;
they are not public domain and not CC0.

| Group | Instruments |
|---|---|
| 弦樂 | 小提琴 violin, 中提琴 viola, 大提琴 cello, 倍大提琴 double bass |
| 木管 | 長笛 flute, 單簧管 clarinet, 雙簧管 oboe, 巴松管 bassoon |
| 銅管 | 小號 trumpet, 長號 trombone, 圓號 French horn, 大號 tuba |
| 敲擊 | 三角鐵 triangle, 大鼓 bass drum, 響板 castanets (handle castanet), 刮瓜 güiro |

Processing only (no redrawing), with `tools/cutout.py`:
- Existing transparency kept where the file had it (violin, viola, cello, double bass, flute); otherwise the white
  background was removed by flood-fill from the image edges, so white highlights/eyes inside the drawings stay.
- Open gaps between parts were made see-through region by region, chosen by hand:
  brass (`tools/brass-gaps.sh`: trombone slide, trumpet loops, horn coils, tuba loop) and percussion
  (`tools/percussion-gaps.sh`: the triangle's open interior – the face stays floating inside – and the pocket
  between beater and frame). The bass drum, castanets and güiro had no enclosed white gaps to clear.
- Stray specks removed, see-through pinholes inside the drawings filled, edges cleaned (no white halo),
  trimmed, resized to max 1024 px.

Real-world sizes used for default scaling (1 unit = 1 mm, in `instruments.js`): violin ≈59 cm, viola ≈67 cm,
cello ≈130 cm, double bass ≈190 cm (incl. endpins); flute ≈67 cm, clarinet ≈66 cm, oboe ≈65 cm, bassoon ≈134 cm;
trumpet ≈50 cm and trombone ≈120 cm (along the diagonal), horn ≈60 cm across, tuba ≈90 cm tall;
triangle ≈20 cm, bass drum head ≈80 cm, handle castanet ≈25 cm, güiro ≈40 cm. Very small instruments start at a
minimum visible size so children can see and grab them.
